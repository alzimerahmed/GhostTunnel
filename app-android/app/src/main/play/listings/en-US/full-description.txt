Protect your online privacy with GhostTunnel, a smart VPN client for Android.

HOW GHOSTTUNNEL WORKS

GhostTunnel for Android includes essential VPN profile import and connection features.

INCLUDED FEATURES:

- Support for OpenVPN and WireGuard protocols.
- Import and manage VPN profiles.
- OpenVPN obfuscation (using --scramble).

OpenVPN support focuses on common options; compression is not supported because it is not secure.

PRIVACY YOU CAN TRUST

GhostTunnel is built with transparency and privacy in mind:
- No ads, trackers, or unnecessary background activities—just the VPN connection you need.
