Protect your online privacy with GhostTunnel, a smart VPN client seamlessly integrated with tvOS.

You can use the iOS or macOS app to set up and manage VPN profiles, or upload profiles directly from a web browser over HTTP. iOS and macOS are not required to use GhostTunnel on Apple TV.

HOW GHOSTTUNNEL WORKS ON APPLE TV

The base Apple TV app includes time-limited connections. To remove the time limit and unlock the full Apple TV experience, an in-app purchase is required.

FAMILY SHARING ENABLED

All purchases support Family Sharing, so you can share them with your family members.

PRIVACY YOU CAN TRUST

GhostTunnel is built with transparency and privacy in mind:
- See exactly which servers you’re connecting to.
- No ads, trackers, or unnecessary background activities—just the VPN connection you need.

COMPATIBILITY

OpenVPN support focuses on common options; compression is not supported because it is not secure.

OPEN SOURCE AND COMMUNITY-DRIVEN

GhostTunnel is open source and constantly evolving with the help of its community. Check out the project on GitHub: https://github.com/alzimerahmed/GhostTunnel
